Smart Job Tracker — free AI job application tracker

Smart Job Tracker is a free web app for managing a job search. Track every application from Applied through OA, Interview, and Offer; match your resume against job descriptions with an explainable score that lists matched and missing skills; discover postings from Greenhouse, Lever, and Ashby career pages; connect Gmail to detect interview invites and rejections; and get interview and follow-up reminders by email, with WhatsApp coming soon.
