Privacy & Data Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Smart Job Tracker is an independent web application for organizing a job search. This policy explains, in specific terms, what information we collect, why we collect it, how optional Google services work, who receives information, how long we retain it, and how you can delete it.

Short version: Google Sign-In is optional and uses only basic account-profile data. Gmail and Google Calendar access are separate, optional connections. We request limited access for job-search features, do not sell Google user data, do not use it for advertising, and let you disconnect integrations from your profile.

1. Who operates the service

Smart Job Tracker is operated as an independent software project. The service website is https://smartjobtracker.indevs.in/. Privacy questions and data requests are handled at hasanryan052@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

Account and authentication data

For an email account, we collect your email address and a securely hashed password. We never store your plain-text password. For Google Sign-In, we receive the basic profile information described below, such as your email address, display name, and Google account identifier.

Job-search information

We store information you choose to enter or save, including company names, role titles, job descriptions, application status, notes, dates, reminders, skills, experience, education, preferred roles, location preferences, and profile information.

Resume and generated content

When you upload a resume, we extract text and profile information for matching, tailoring, resume building, and interview preparation. The original uploaded file is not retained after extraction unless the product specifically tells you otherwise. AI-generated suggestions are associated with your account only as needed to provide the feature.

Technical and usage data

We may process security and diagnostic logs such as IP address, request path, timestamps, device/browser information, and error details. We do not use advertising trackers or sell browsing activity.

3. Google user data and APIs

Google integrations are optional. We request the minimum access needed for the selected feature and handle Google user data in accordance with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Google Sign-In

When you select “Continue with Google,” Google provides OpenID Connect account information: your Google account identifier, email address, email-verification status, display name, and profile image when available. We use this information only to create, authenticate, and display your Smart Job Tracker account. Google Sign-In does not by itself give Smart Job Tracker access to Gmail, Google Calendar, Drive, contacts, or other Google content.

Gmail connection

If you separately choose “Connect Gmail,” we request the Gmail gmail.readonly scope. This allows the service to read messages needed to identify recruitment-related messages, such as interview invitations, rejections, and status updates. We do not send, edit, delete, label, or compose Gmail messages. We store only the message identifiers and job-related fields needed for your tracker and review queue, together with encrypted OAuth tokens needed to refresh the connection.

Google Calendar connection

For interview detection, we may request read-only Calendar access to identify relevant events. When you explicitly ask Smart Job Tracker to create a reminder event, the service may request permission to create that event. Calendar information is used only for interview and reminder features; it is not sold, used for advertising, or used to build an unrelated profile.

Google user-data processing and sharing

Google data is used only to provide the connected feature: authenticate your account, find recruitment emails, prepare status-review suggestions, identify interview events, and create reminders you request. If email classification is enabled, the minimum message content needed for classification may be sent to Google Gemini as a service provider. It is not used for advertising, sold, or used to determine credit, insurance, employment eligibility, or other unrelated decisions.

We do not sell Google user data. We do not share it with employers, data brokers, advertisers, or unrelated third parties. We do not transfer Google user data for a purpose unrelated to providing or improving the Smart Job Tracker features that you requested.

4. How information is used

  • Authenticate accounts and protect the service.
  • Track applications and show status history.
  • Match resumes and skills with job descriptions and display explainable scores.
  • Discover public job postings from configured sources.
  • Send reminders that you create by email or connected calendar services.
  • Provide resume, interview-preparation, and tailoring features.
  • Detect errors, prevent abuse, maintain reliability, and improve the service.

We do not sell, rent, or use personal data or Google user data for targeted advertising.

5. Sharing and service providers

We may use infrastructure and service providers for hosting, database storage, email delivery, job discovery, and AI processing. They may process information only to provide services to Smart Job Tracker, under appropriate confidentiality and security obligations. We do not share personal data with employers or job boards merely because you use the tracker. Public job listings link to the original posting, where you apply directly.

We may disclose information if required by law, to protect users or the service from fraud or abuse, or as part of a lawful change in ownership. We do not make automated hiring decisions; match scores and AI suggestions are estimates for your review.

6. Security

Traffic is served over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed. OAuth tokens for Gmail and Calendar are encrypted at rest and are not included in data exports. Access is restricted by account and role. No security system is perfect, so please contact us promptly if you believe your account or data has been compromised.

7. Retention and deletion

Account, application, resume-derived, and profile data is retained while your account is active so the service can operate. Server security logs are retained for up to 30 days. Aggregated statistics without personal identifiers may be retained for service improvement.

You can disconnect Gmail or Calendar from your Profile at any time. Disconnecting stops future access and deletes the stored connection tokens. You can delete your account from account settings or request deletion by email. Account deletion removes personal data and connected Google-derived records within 30 days, except for information we must retain by law. Stored OAuth tokens are revoked where Google permits revocation.

8. Your choices and rights

You can choose email login instead of Google Sign-In, decline optional integrations, disconnect Gmail or Calendar, review or correct profile data, export available account data, and delete your account. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object to, or receive a portable copy of your personal data. Email hasanryan052@gmail.com to make a request; we aim to respond within five business days.

9. Cookies and local storage

The app uses first-party browser storage for authentication, cookie-choice status, theme preference, saved job actions, recent-visit state, and sidebar preference. These mechanisms are required for the app experience and are not advertising cookies. You can clear them through your browser settings; clearing the authentication token signs you out.

10. Contact and policy changes

For privacy questions, Google-data questions, access requests, deletion requests, or concerns about this policy, contact hasanryan052@gmail.com. We will update this page when our data practices change and will show the latest revision date at the top.
